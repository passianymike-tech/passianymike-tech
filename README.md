### About Me

I'm a **Senior GIS Analyst** with 5+ years of experience in geospatial data analysis, remote sensing, and GIS application development. I hold a **Master of Science in Geographic Information Systems** from the University of Nairobi and a **Bachelor of Applied Science in Geo-Informatics** from the Technical University of Kenya.

My work spans environmental monitoring, urban planning, disaster management, and agricultural applications — delivering evidence-based spatial solutions for government agencies, NGOs, and research institutions across East Africa.
